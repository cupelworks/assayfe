Why these shapes

Inconclusive is hollow because it isn't a warning: 28 passes out of 28 is Inconclusive at 90% only for lack of runs.

Incomplete is half-filled: the batch was stopped. What ran is kept, with verdicts where enough ran.

Done is framed: a trial finished. The square is closed, and its centre holds what the trial learned about each check - but a trial gives no verdict.

A run keeps its own words - Green, Amber, Red. Passed and Failed are said only of a batch and its checks.
